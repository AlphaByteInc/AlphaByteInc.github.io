Life & Health Insurance Exam Prep (iOS / Android app)

About the App

Original practice questions for the Life and Health insurance producer licensing exam: 680 questions with explanations for every choice (520 General Life & Health questions and 160 Texas state law questions), a 428-term glossary, a Texas State Law Drill, and full-format Texas mock exams (130 questions, 150 minutes). The App works offline.

Version 1 includes a Texas state law pack only. The General questions cover topics common to Life and Health exams, but other states' insurance laws are not covered.

The actual exam uses a scaled score, and the passing score is a scaled score of 70. This is not the same as answering 70% of questions correctly. The App's mock exams show a simple percentage and use 70% only as a practice pass mark. The real exam also includes 15 unscored pretest questions, which the mock exams do not reproduce.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same App Store or Google Play account, open Settings in the App and tap "Restore purchase".

Is this a subscription?

No. Full Access is a one-time purchase with no expiration.

Can I move my study history to another device?

No. Study history is stored only on the device where you studied.

Which states are supported?

Texas only in version 1. Other states are shown as "Coming soon" and cannot be selected or purchased. You can choose "Skip for now (General only)" to study the General questions without a state pack.

Is this an official app?

No. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Texas Department of Insurance or any other state department of insurance, Pearson VUE, or PSI. Practice questions are original and are not actual exam questions.

Which outline and law does the content follow?

The Pearson VUE Texas Life and Health Agent content outline effective September 1, 2026, and Texas insurance law as of October 2026. Exam content and rules can change. Always confirm current details with the Texas Department of Insurance and Pearson VUE.

Does the App guarantee that I will pass?

No. The App is a study aid and does not guarantee exam results.
