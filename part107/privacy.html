Privacy Policy

Part 107 Exam Prep (iOS / Android app)

This policy explains how 株式会社アルファバイト (AlphaByte) ("we") handles information in the mobile app "Part 107 Exam Prep" (the "App").

The App is an independent study aid for the FAA Remote Pilot (Part 107) knowledge test. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the FAA.

  • The App does not collect personal information or any other data about you.
  • Your study history is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • There is no account registration, advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive your payment information.

1. Information we collect

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage data or crash data. We do not operate servers for the App, and the App sends no information to us. The App works offline, including its built-in chart excerpts and figures.

2. Information stored on your device

So that you can continue studying, the App stores the following only on your device:

  • Your answers, whether they were correct, and accuracy by Area and ACS Task
  • Your review list, mock exam results, and any practice session or mock exam in progress
  • Whether you have made the in-app purchase

This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can erase your study history with "Reset Study History" in Settings. Deleting the App deletes all of this information. Study history is therefore not transferred to a new device (your purchase can be restored with "Restore Purchases").

3. In-app purchases

The one-time purchase is handled through the App Store (Apple Inc.) or Google Play (Google LLC). Those companies handle your payment information; we do not receive card numbers or information that identifies you. The App communicates with the store only to check whether the purchase has been made. See each company's privacy policy:

  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Sharing with third parties

Because the App does not collect your information, we do not share or sell it. The App contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

5. Links to other websites

The App's About screen links to public sources (for example, the FAA's Airman Certification Standards and VFR chart pages, the PSI applicant information bulletin, and 14 CFR Parts 89 and 107 on eCFR) and to these legal pages. They open in your browser, and those websites' own policies apply.

6. Children

The App is intended for people preparing for the FAA Remote Pilot (Part 107) knowledge test. It does not collect information from anyone, regardless of age.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control the data stored on your device and can erase it at any time as described in section 2.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when laws or the App's features change. Significant changes will be announced on this page or on the App's store listing. Updated versions take effect when posted on this page.
