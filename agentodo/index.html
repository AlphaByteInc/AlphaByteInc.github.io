AgenTodo

A to-do app that hands your to-dos to AI. iPhone, iPad and Mac. By AlphaByte, Inc.

AgenTodo is a to-do app. Write a to-do, tap "Do this" (これやって), and hand it to an AI agent you already use: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or Cursor on your own Mac, or a webhook destination you set up yourself. Progress shows up in the to-do's chat thread and in a Live Activity on the Lock Screen. You can add to-dos by voice, from the widget or with Siri. It also works as a plain to-do app with no AI connected. Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Features

  • To-dos, lists, tags and free notes, synced between your devices.
  • Hand a to-do to AI on your Mac or to your own webhook, and reply in a chat thread.
  • Lock Screen Live Activities and Home Screen widgets.
  • Add by voice. Speech recognition runs on your device.
  • Optional suggestions from your calendar and Gmail, made by the on-device AI.

How AgenTodo uses Google user data

Connecting Gmail is optional. If you connect it, AgenTodo asks Google for read-only access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). The app connects directly from your iPhone or iPad to Google, reads the subject, sender and the first lines of recent received messages, and uses the on-device AI to suggest to-dos such as "reply to this invoice" or "book this appointment". You choose which suggestions to add.

  • Email content is never sent to or stored on our servers. It is processed only on your device.
  • Google credentials are kept only in the device keychain.
  • We do not use Gmail data for advertising, do not sell it, do not use it to train AI models, and do not let humans read it.
  • You can disconnect Gmail at any time in the app, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AgenTodo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
