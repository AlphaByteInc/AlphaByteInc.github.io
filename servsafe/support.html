ServSafe Manager Exam Prep (iOS / Android app)

About the App

Original practice questions for the ServSafe Food Protection Manager (Certified Food Protection Manager) exam: 600 questions with explanations for every choice, a 300+ term glossary, and real-format mock exams (90 questions, 120 minutes). The App works offline.

The real exam has 90 questions, including 10 unscored pilot questions. The passing score is 70% of the scored questions (56 of 80). The App's mock exams score all 90 questions, and 70% (63 of 90) is the passing line.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same App Store or Google Play account, open Settings in the App and tap "Restore Purchases".

Can I move my study history to another device?

No. Study history is stored only on the device where you studied.

Is this the official ServSafe app?

No. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the National Restaurant Association or ServSafe. Practice questions are original and are not actual exam questions. ServSafe is a registered trademark of the National Restaurant Association.

Which rules does the content follow?

The U.S. FDA Food Code (2022). State and local rules can differ, and certificate validity periods and training requirements may vary by jurisdiction. Always follow your local regulatory authority.
