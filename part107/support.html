Part 107 Exam Prep (iOS / Android app)

About the App

Original practice questions for the FAA Remote Pilot (Part 107) initial knowledge test (UAG): 600 three-choice questions with explanations for every choice, each tagged with its ACS code and source; a 347-term glossary; and 8 full-length mock exams (60 questions, 120 minutes, 70% to pass). 150 questions use a figure, including 90 sectional-chart drills on excerpts of current FAA VFR sectional charts with pinch zoom and full screen. The App works offline.

Mock exams use a fixed number of questions per Area that follows the current PSI blueprint (effective September 29, 2025): Regulations 29, Airspace 12, Weather 3, Loading and Performance 2, Operations 14. The real test also includes unscored questions, which the mock exams do not reproduce.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same App Store or Google Play account, open Settings in the App and tap "Restore Purchases".

Is this a subscription?

No. Full Access is a one-time purchase with no expiration. The App has no ads.

What is free?

10 questions in each of the 5 Areas (50 questions) and the first mock exam.

Can I move my study history to another device?

No. Study history is stored only on the device where you studied.

Can I use the chart excerpts for flying?

No. Chart excerpts are for training only — not for navigation. Always use current charts for flight.

Is this an official app?

No. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the FAA. Practice questions are original and are not actual FAA test questions.

Which standards does the content follow?

The Remote Pilot Airman Certification Standards (FAA-S-ACS-10B), the PSI UAG blueprint effective September 29, 2025, and regulations current as of October 2026. Test format, fees and rules can change. Always confirm current details with the FAA and PSI.

Does the App guarantee that I will pass?

No. The App is a study aid and does not guarantee test results.
